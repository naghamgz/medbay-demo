More Than a Medical Supplier

MedBay International is a healthcare technology partner — supporting hospitals, clinics and specialists with trusted equipment and dependable service.

Who We Are

A Healthcare Technology Partner

MedBay International is a global medical technology and healthcare solutions company specializing in the sourcing, distribution, manufacturing, and development of medical devices, surgical instruments, and healthcare consumables. Founded and led by Dr. Abdelrahman Elashrafi, an anesthesia consultant and healthcare business development leader, MedBay operates across Egypt, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the United States, and China, with manufacturing partnerships in Egypt and China.
