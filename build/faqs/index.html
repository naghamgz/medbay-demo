Answers to common questions about ordering from MedBay.

How do I change my account's default billing and shipping addresses?

Access "Address Book" or "Manage Addresses," then use "Add New Address" to create additional entries. During checkout, select your shipping address, then uncheck "My billing and shipping address are the same" on the payment page to choose different billing options from your saved addresses.
